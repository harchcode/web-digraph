How to use

  • Generate: Enter a number in the top right to instantly generate massive grid layouts for testing performance.
  • Move Mode: Click and drag nodes to reposition them.
  • Create Mode: Click empty space to spawn a node. Drag from one node to another to connect them.
  • Multi Select: Click and drag on the canvas to draw a selection box.
  • Hotkeys: Hold Shift to temporarily toggle between Move and Create modes, and Single and Multi select.
  • Delete: Press Backspace or Delete to remove selected items.
